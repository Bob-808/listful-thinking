How to Play

Listful Thinking is a moderator‑driven trivia game where players answer verbally.

It is not for the faint of heart. Each question more daunting than the last.

Game Flow

  • The moderator reads the question aloud.
  • Players take turns providing answers.
  • Correct answers are revealed on screen.
  • Incorrect answers may eliminate players depending on mode.

Game Modes

Multiplayer: Players compete until only one remains.

Challenge Mode: Moderator attempts to reveal all answers with limited misses.

Tips

Use the Next Question button to advance, and the Wrong Answer button to track penalties.
