Games like Trivial Pursuit allow a dominant player to monopolize the board and the other players have to sit around doing nothing until that person gets one wrong. This game makes one player the moderator for one round. They hold the phone or tablet so the others cannot see the screen (can also be managed with a laptop but is a bit trickier). They read the question and then each player gets one turn to provide one answer in the list. If they are wrong they are out of the round only. The moderator clicks the right answer to draw a line through it. Obviously, answers cannot be repeated. When everyone has a turn, the cycle repeats with the remaining players for that round until either the answers are all given or no player is left. The moderator hands the job over to the next player and they repeat for the next question.
